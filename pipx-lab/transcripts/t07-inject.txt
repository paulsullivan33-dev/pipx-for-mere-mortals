Plain pipx list does not itemise guests; the record lives in
the venv's own metadata file:

$ python3 -c "import json; m=json.load(open('/home/hatch/.local/share/pipx/venvs/pycowsay/pipx_metadata.json')); print(list(m['injected_packages']))"
['cowsay']
[exit 0]
